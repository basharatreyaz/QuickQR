1. Overview & Principles

QuickQR ("we", "our", or "us") provides high-performance dynamic QR code routing, vector generation, and telemetry services. We design our systems according to the principle of Privacy by Design and Default.

We do not sell personal data, do not build cross-site behavioural profiles of scanners, and do not employ invasive fingerprinting or third-party advertising tracking libraries.

2. Information We Collect

We process information in two distinct roles: as an Account Provider (for registered users) and as a Dynamic Routing Provider (for visitors scanning QR codes).

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, password hash (hashed via bcrypt with high work factor), and plan preference.
  • QR Campaign Configuration: We store the target destinations you configure (URLs, Wi-Fi keys, vCard contacts, emails), styling parameters (hex colors, error correction, margins), and custom names.
  • Billing Data: If you upgrade to a paid subscription, payment transactions are securely handled directly by verified payment partners (such as PayPal). We store transaction IDs, subscription status, and billing cycle dates, but never store credit card numbers or banking secrets.

3. QR Scan Telemetry & Zero Raw-IP Storage

GDPR Anonymization Guarantee: QuickQR NEVER stores raw IP addresses in the database. All incoming scan requests are cryptographically salted and hashed using one-way SHA-256 before storage.

When an end user scans a dynamic QR code pointing to /r/{short_code}, our edge router logs minimal, aggregate telemetry:

  • Cryptographic IP Hash: The IP address is hashed: SHA256(IP + System_Salt). This allows deduplication of daily unique scans without revealing the visitor's true identity.
  • Device Category & Platform: Parsed from the HTTP User-Agent header (e.g. Mobile, iOS, Chrome Mobile).
  • Approximate Country & Region: Derived from non-identifying network headers (e.g. United States, Germany) for demographic reporting.
  • HTTP Referrer: Originating browser referrer if provided.
  • Timestamp: Date and time of scan for traffic volume analysis.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

QuickQR uses strictly necessary functional cookies required for platform security and authentication:

  • PHPSESSID: Session cookie to keep your dashboard login authenticated.
  • theme_preference: Optional local preference for dark/light mode display.

We do NOT set third-party marketing cookies, Google AdSense cookies, Facebook Pixel beacons, or session recording spyware.

5. How We Use Data

We process your data strictly to fulfill the contract of service:

  • Operating the dynamic redirect router with sub-15ms latency.
  • Rendering scan analytics, timeline graphs, and device breakdowns in your user dashboard.
  • Protecting accounts with brute-force rate limiters and DDoS mitigation.
  • Generating vector SVG and high-resolution PNG QR images on demand.

6. Third Parties & Data Processors

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your information. We engage only reputable infrastructure providers bound by rigorous Data Processing Agreements (DPA):

  • Payment Gateways: PayPal REST API for subscription billing tokenization.
  • CDN & Web Infrastructure: Secure hosting environments with DDoS protection.

7. Your GDPR & Privacy Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full control over your information:

  • Right to Access: You can view all stored campaigns and analytics directly in your dashboard or request a full data dump.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit campaign names, target URLs, and account profile anytime in real time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Deleting a QR code removes it immediately. Account termination deletes all associated records.
  • Data Portability: Export scan analytics in standard JSON / CSV formats and export QR codes in universal SVG vector format.

8. Security Infrastructure

Our application implements modern defense-in-depth security measures:

  • All transport channels enforce TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Bcrypt algorithm with work factor 12 for password hashing.
  • 100% prepared SQL statements to prevent SQL injection vulnerabilities.
  • Strict destination URL sanitization blocking javascript:, data:, and malicious schemes.
  • Automated IP-based and token-based rate limiting against brute force attempts.
