Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dynamic QR codes, vector SVG printing, scan analytics, and plan upgrades.

Static QR codes encode the destination payload directly into the matrix pattern: once printed, it can never be altered. Dynamic QR codes encode a lightweight, cryptographically secure short link (such as /r/smr2026k) that forwards scanners to your desired destination. You can change the target URL anytime in your dashboard without reprinting stickers, packaging, or marketing collateral.
Yes! You can use our instant playground on the homepage to generate, customize colors, and download high-resolution PNG or SVG codes without signing up. If you want scan tracking, editable destination URLs, and saved campaigns, you can register for our free account anytime.
Dynamic QR codes on active plans never expire unless you intentionally set an expiration date in the campaign settings or choose to pause them. Scanners will always be redirected smoothly without interruption.
Standard raster formats like PNG and JPG become pixelated and blurry when scaled up for large printing. Vector SVG encodes the QR code as mathematical XML vectors, allowing you to scale it to any dimension—from a 1-inch product label to a 50-foot billboard—with absolute razor sharpness and 100% scan fidelity.
Yes! QuickQR provides a visual color picker and hex code input for both foreground and background colors. We recommend maintaining high contrast (such as a dark foreground on a white or light background) to ensure rapid optical scanning across all camera lenses.
QuickQR never stores raw IP addresses in the database. Incoming scan requests are hashed using a one-way salted SHA-256 algorithm: SHA256(IP + Salt). This allows us to report daily unique scan counts without retaining personal identifiers. We also extract non-identifying platform specs (e.g. iOS vs Android) and approximate countries.
We never break your printed QR codes. If your campaign exceeds the monthly limit, redirects continue to resolve smoothly while an upgrade alert is presented in your dashboard. You can upgrade to Pro (50,000 scans) or Business (1,000,000 scans) anytime.
Yes, there are no lock-in contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription whenever you wish from the Plans & Billing page.
